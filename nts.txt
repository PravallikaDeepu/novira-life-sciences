Dr. Girish Ganesh is a Clinical Research Fellow in IBD Gastroenterology with extensive experience in clinical research, gastroenterology-focused clinical studies, inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) research, clinical trial operations, and translational medical research. He is also the Founder & Managing Director of Novira Life Sciences, a clinical research organization supporting clinical operations, healthcare research activities, and scientific collaboration across the UK, Europe, and international regions.

He holds an MD (Medicine) degree along with an MSc in Cancer Research and Precision Oncology from the University of Glasgow, UK. He also completed his Clinical Research Fellowship in IBD Gastroenterology, where his work focused on inflammatory bowel disease research, gastroenterology clinical studies, patient-centered clinical research activities, clinical trial-related work, scientific collaboration, and evidence-based healthcare research.

During his Clinical Research Fellowship in IBD Gastroenterology, Dr. Girish was actively involved in clinical research activities related to autoimmune gastrointestinal diseases, patient recruitment and coordination, clinical documentation, protocol-based research activities, scientific data collection, and multidisciplinary healthcare research environments aligned with ICH-GCP standards. His experience also includes exposure to biologics, inflammatory bowel disease management, translational research approaches, and clinical trial coordination within gastroenterology-focused research settings.

In addition to his IBD Gastroenterology research background, he also has experience in oncology and precision medicine research, clinical data management, regulatory documentation, healthcare research operations, and scientific communication. His work includes involvement in clinical trial workflows, research coordination, medical research support activities, and international collaborative research environments.

Through Novira Life Sciences, Dr. Girish is involved in supporting clinical research operations, clinical trial support activities, clinical data management, scientific consulting, healthcare research coordination, and clinical research training initiatives. He is also actively engaged in mentoring students and professionals interested in clinical research, CRO operations, and life sciences careers.
